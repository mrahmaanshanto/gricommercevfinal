Every state answers four questions

What happened
“Invoices could not load”, not “Something went wrong”.
Why, if known
Name the service, the store or the filter; never a stack trace.
What to do
One primary action: retry, clear filters, ask for access.
What did not happen
Say nothing was changed or sent, whenever a write was in flight.
