Rules

  • The owner creates the PIN; staff can never create one.
  • A PIN works once, within 10 minutes, for the staff member who enters it.
  • The owner picks the time (30, 60 or 120 minutes) and the areas that can change.
  • Everything outside those areas is view-only.
  • The owner can end the session from their admin at any time.
  • Every page and change is recorded with the staff member's name.
