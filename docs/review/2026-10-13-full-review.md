## Online selling
**Verdict:** The order operation is strong: the verify → approve → pack → courier flow, the Order work queues, the courier statement and courier returns all work. The Online Store and customer-facing side is mostly static mock-up, and two return and checkout paths are broken. **Features:** 3/5 · **UX:** 3/5

### Keep (works well)
- The order page's next-step card: verify by call or auto call, approve or take advance + approve, packing checklist that marks the order Ready for courier, send to courier. Steps run once only, and Edit order previews its effect on stock, money and courier before saving.
- Order work: the courier review with per-order checks, the pick list grouped by product with bins, and background bulk courier booking and label printing with per-order Retry.
- Courier statement and Courier returns tie COD money to each parcel: partial receive, good vs damaged stock, refund on paid orders, payout due and money the courier is holding.
- Blog (posts, editor with autosave restore, SEO score and publish checks, categories, authors) and Sales channels' plain-word sync issues with a fix and retry are solid and use the shared design system.
